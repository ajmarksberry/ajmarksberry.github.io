Case Study 03

Reach: AI Booking Platform

Designing an entire dual-sided platform from the ground up, an AI-driven consumer booking experience and a business analytics dashboard for beauty & wellness studios.

Client
Reach (Contract)
Role
Design Lead · End to end
Platform
B2C & B2B · Mobile & Desktop
Year
2019 – 2021
Tools
Figma, Sketch

Reach is an AI-powered platform that helps beauty & wellness businesses re-engage clients, last-minute booking, targeted promotions, waitlist management, and client communication. But it's not just a booking tool: it's a two-sided platform.

When I joined as Design Lead, neither side existed. Over the engagement I designed the entire product from the ground up, the consumer-facing AI booking flow and client portal, and the business-facing analytics dashboard and management tools, with a complete visual language and atomic component system built from scratch.

The Challenge

Two connected problems, one design language.

Beauty & wellness businesses face two interconnected problems. Last-minute cancellations leave revenue-generating slots unfilled, and the traditional response is slow and impersonal. And even when a platform promises AI-driven re-engagement, owners need to see the evidence: what revenue did the AI generate, and which campaigns drove it?

The consumer experience had to feel human, spoken in the provider's voice, personalized by client history, graceful across dozens of edge cases. The business experience had to be clear, data-rich, and actionable. Both sides needed one coherent visual language, built from a completely blank canvas.

Consumer side
Personal, trustworthy AI booking, voiced by the provider.
Business side
Clear, data-rich proof of exactly what the AI earned.
The Solution

Design language first, then both sides at once.

I established the complete visual system from scratch, then built the consumer and business products simultaneously on top of it.

00

The design language & atomic system

A 3-level typographic system, a comprehensive Figma component library, expandable cards, date/time pickers, slot grids, provider avatar lists, price breakdowns, step indicators, data tables, bar charts, upload/crop flows, and full error & empty states, designed across every breakpoint: 1200, 1024, 768, and 375px.

Color semantics
  • Blue: interactive elements & primary CTAs
  • Amber: discounts & positive financial highlights
  • Green: success & additive actions
  • Pink / red: reserved exclusively for errors & urgency
Consumer side B2C · Mobile & Desktop
  1. 01

    AI-powered booking flow

    A 3-step experience, Find → Review → Confirm, that surfaced differently for each client. 6+ personalized AI entry states covered returning clients with active offers, expired and redeemed offers, a departed provider, welcome-back, and brand-new clients.

    • →Book by service (typeahead + accordion + tabs) or by provider
    • →Date/time picker, AM/PM segmentation, 15-minute slot grid
    • →Payment countdown timer in red, the sole use of that color
  2. 02

    Waitlist & client account portal

    An unobtrusive “Add me to the waitlist” flow with Text or Email notification and a green CTA, semantically distinct from booking blue, signaling an additive action. Plus a full account portal: personalized home, My Appointments with a three-state refund pipeline, and granular email & SMS preferences.

    Dynamic Handlebars HTML email templates, personalized per client, offer, provider, and salon.

Business side B2B · Desktop dashboard
  1. 03

    Results dashboard & revenue analytics

    A data-rich desktop dashboard giving owners visibility into exactly how the AI performed, top-level metric cards, a four-step Reach → Open → Click → Book funnel, and orange bar charts attributing revenue.

    • →Reach-Driven Bookings vs. Service Upsells, clear revenue split
    • →Revenue Details drill-downs, sortable transaction tables
    • →Empty states and responsive down to a 200px widget
  2. 04

    Business & provider management

    My Business (info & hours), My Profile (owner details, password with error states), and My Providers, a provider list with availability status indicators, individual profiles, and a complete avatar upload flow: initials preview, drag-and-drop upload, progress, crop tool, and error state. Fully responsive across every breakpoint.

Results

A complete platform, shipped.

Both sides of the platform shipped and were used by real beauty & wellness businesses and their clients, delivered with full responsive coverage, complete edge-case handling, and a single coherent design language built entirely from scratch.

  • ✓ Both sides shipped to real users: consumer AI booking through to business revenue analytics
  • ✓ Designed from a blank canvas: complete visual language and atomic system across all breakpoints
  • ✓ Every edge, empty & error state resolved: the full range of senior product design, consumer and business
  • ◷ Conversion, waitlist & revenue metrics: to be confirmed and added once available
