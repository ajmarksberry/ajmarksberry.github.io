About

A senior product designer who owns the whole arc, from the fuzzy front end of a problem to the accessible, production-ready pixel that ships.

Over 26 years I've led design across B2B platforms and consumer products, facilitating design sprints, building and maintaining atomic design systems, running moderated research, and delivering fully annotated, WCAG-compliant handoffs. I work best embedded with a team, close to research and engineering.

Today I design for Expedia's Travel Agent Affiliate Program (TAAP) and continue to run No One Creative, the consultancy I founded in 2001.

Focus areas

Product design, design systems, accessibility (WCAG 2.2), design sprints, UX research & prototyping.
