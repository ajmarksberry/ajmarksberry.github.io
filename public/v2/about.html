About

AJ Marksberry

I’m a Senior Product Designer with 27+ years of experience helping organizations transform complex business challenges into intuitive, human-centered digital products.

Over the years, I’ve worked across travel, healthcare, insurance, communications, hospitality, ecommerce, and emerging technology industries, partnering closely with Product, Engineering, Research, Content Design, and business stakeholders to bring ideas from discovery through delivery.

Most recently, I was a contract product designer at Expedia, working on the Travel Agent Affiliate Program (TAAP), which supports over 200,000 travel advisors across 35 countries. It was exciting to be part of Expedia’s fast-growing B2B business, where revenue was growing around 25% year-over-year. My work focused on improving itinerary management, booking workflows, and post-booking experiences, working closely with Product, Engineering, Research, and Content teams along the way.
